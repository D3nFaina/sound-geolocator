With ✎ Trace, click 4 to 8 points roughly along the line and press Enter (or double-click): only the narrow band around your points is digitised, so it can't wander onto other lines. Check the dots sit on the line, remove strays with ⌫ Erase (drag a box), then Fit. Everything is saved and comes back next time.     Every 2 km cell and start time where real flights match each ticked pass (speed and closest distance within the fit errors), then the silence filter: a place is dropped when another aircraft came within the slant distance below during the quiet stretch between two passes.
